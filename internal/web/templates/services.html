Background jobs

Desired activation Active interval and cron services submit tasks on schedule and can also subscribe to external events; event services respond to matched Task outcomes and custom events; continuous services maintain managed work; manual services wait for a trigger; webhook and external-signal services accept deliveries. Tasks use their configured pools and service concurrency limits. Service priority affects priority pools; other pool disciplines retain their ordering. Disabling a service stops future submissions. Continuous services also stop their managed task. Other submitted tasks continue.
